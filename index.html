CrossPaths

Find where you and a friend were in the same place at the same time.

Private by design. Photos and files are read inside this page, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is kept once you close the tab.

Step 1 Add each person's history

Use photos (they need a GPS tag), a Google Maps Timeline export, or a trail file your friend sent you. You can mix them.

Step 3 Where you crossed paths

    Getting accurate results

    iPhone photos. Tap Add photos, choose Photo Library, then tap Options at the bottom left and switch Location on before you pick. Without that, iOS removes the GPS tag and the photo is skipped. Pick a month or an event at a time if Safari struggles with big selections; each batch adds to the last.

    Google Maps Timeline. In the Google Maps app, open your profile, then Settings, then Personal content (or Location), and export your Timeline data. Add the saved .json file here. Menu names change between app versions.

    Two phones. Each person opens this page on their own phone, adds their history, then uses Send my trail to share a small file with only times and places. The other person adds it under Friend. Delete the file afterwards.

    How a match is decided. One of your records and one of theirs must fall inside both the distance and the time you set. Photo times use the time zone stored in the photo, so people in different countries still line up. Timeline visits are filled in every 5 minutes, and routes between places are estimated, so those matches are labelled and rated lower.

    What a match means. It is evidence that you were near each other, not proof that you met. A person with no photos or timeline around a moment cannot match at that moment, so a missing crossing does not mean it did not happen.
